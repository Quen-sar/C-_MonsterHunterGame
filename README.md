# C-_MonsterHunterGame
A turn-based fighting game where your hero must slay a monster. Intended to practise calculations as which type of attack that can be used is determined by whether the monster's health in divisible by 5 or a prime number.

How to play:
Enter a valid username and press the start button.
A monster will be chosen at random as an enemy.
To choose an attack, determine if the monster’s health fits the criteria for spear stab(multiple of 5), arrow shot(prime number) or sword slash(default attack always hits). And click the respective button.
Hint: Mind the damage values displayed in the combat log to find out which type of attack does the most damage on average to a specific monster.
The previous top scores will be listed on the right side of the game interface, try to win a battle with the most health remaining!
